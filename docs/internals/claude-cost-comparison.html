Caveats that matter

Your Claude-side burn will be higher than $183

Composer 2.5 — your daily driver — is unmetered on Cursor and invisible in the $183.26. On Claude, everything (Claude Code + claude.ai chat) draws from one pool. Your Grok / Kimi / Sonnet work (~$48) becomes mostly Sonnet 4.6, and Kimi's analysis role presumably becomes Opus or Sonnet-thinking — all of it counting against the same session and weekly limits.

Opus is the lever

74% of your bill is Opus 4.6 ($135.04), mostly thinking sessions. Sonnet 4.6 costs $3/$15 per MTok vs Opus at $5/$25 — a 40% cut. Routing analysis/planning to Sonnet-thinking is what makes Max 5x + credits land near $130–150; keeping the current Opus-thinking habit points squarely at Max 20x.

Fine print on Claude plans

Usage credits past plan caps bill at standard API rates with a configurable monthly spending cap. Weekly limits are running 50% above published levels through Aug 31, 2026 (promo, possibly permanent). API rates: Opus 4.6 $5/$25 per MTok, Sonnet 4.6 $3/$15; cache reads at ~0.1x input. Max tiers are monthly-only; Pro drops to $17/mo billed annually.

Bottom line
